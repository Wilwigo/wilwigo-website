Built around real plans

Travel gets more useful when your friends can see the right parts of it.

Wilwigo connects the trips people are already planning with the people they already know. It is not a public travel feed and it does not track your live GPS location.

01

Add a trip

Save where you are going, when you will be there, and whether that trip is visible to friends or kept hidden.

02

Spot the overlap

Wilwigo brings overlapping city plans together so a coincidence can become dinner, a visit, a weekend plan, or simply a useful heads-up.

03

Keep it in your circle

Your friends, trips, Passport and activity stay focused on the people and plans that matter to you, with clear visibility controls.

Plan in the product

A trip becomes useful the moment you add it.

The guided planner keeps trip creation lightweight: destination, dates, status, visibility and a picture. Once saved, Wilwigo can connect that plan to the rest of your travel circle.

Early access runs on the web today. Native iPhone and Android apps are in development as another way to use the same product — not as a replacement for an interim version.

Your plans are not a live location feed.

Wilwigo uses the home base and trips you choose to enter to infer city-level presence. It does not collect your device GPS or track where you actually are. Trip visibility controls let you decide whether a stay is shared with friends or kept hidden.
